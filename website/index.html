Product Philosophy

AI that belongs to you, not the cloud.

Most mobile assistants send your personal notes, calendar, photos, and thoughts directly to centralized servers for profit and training. SWAYAM-GPT fundamentally flips this paradigm: your memories and documents remain sovereign on your device, hardware-encrypted, and governed by user-controlled policy gates.

Privacy & Data Governance

No Ads. No User Tracking. No Data Brokerage.

We believe in transparent, code-verified privacy. Here is what the application actually does with your data.

Air-Gapped Offline-Only Mode

Toggle one switch in Privacy Center to completely disable all network sockets. The PrivacyEngine will immediately reject any remote call, running 100% locally.

Sanitized Local Telemetry

Analytics in SWAYAM-GPT are stored strictly on-device in memory to render usage charts. All prompts, image pixels, and personal tokens are programmatically stripped.

User-Controlled API Keys

When using Cloud AI fallback, requests are made directly to Google Gemini using the developer API configuration or private gateway. Your conversations are never saved to secondary advertising databases.
